📱 Get Juice on your phone

Install the dashboard as an app: live countdown, one-tap buttons, and notifications when it's your turn.

  1. Open chargebot-tliw.onrender.com in Safari. It has to be Safari.
  2. Tap Share ⬆︎ then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open Juice from your Home Screen and tap Sign in.
  4. In Slack, open ChargeBot's Home tab and tap Get sign-in code. Type the code into Juice.
  5. Tap 🔔 Turn on notifications and allow them.

Notifications need iOS 16.4 or later, and only work in the Home Screen app, not in Safari.

  1. In Slack, open ChargeBot's Home tab and tap Open dashboard. It opens signed in.
  2. In Chrome, tap ⋮ then Install app, or the ⬇ Install app button at the top of the page.
  3. Open Juice and tap 🔔 Turn on notifications.

Signed out in the app? Tap Sign in and use a code from ChargeBot's Home tab in Slack.

  1. In Slack, open ChargeBot's Home tab and click Open dashboard.
  2. In Chrome or Edge, click ⬇ Install app at the top of the page, or the install icon in the address bar.
  3. Click 🔔 Turn on notifications. The buttons on them (like I'm plugged in) work without opening the app.

Each device stays signed in for 90 days. Lost a phone? Use Sign out everywhere in ChargeBot's Home tab.

⏱️ When it's your turn

You get a DM (and a notification, if they're on). Here's the clock:

  1. 0:00The charger is yoursPlug in and tap I'm plugged in. Can't make it? Tap Skip.
  2. 5 minYour 2-hour clock startsEven if you haven't confirmed yet. You get a reminder.
  3. 8 minLast callThe next person gets a heads-up that they might be up.
  4. 10 minTurn passesNo confirmation, so the next person gets it. If nobody's waiting, you can tap Reclaim.

⚡ While you charge

  • A heads-up 10 minutes before the end.
  • Leaving early? Tap I'm done and the next person is told right away.
  • Nobody waiting? Extend by 30 minutes, up to twice.

🏁 Time's up

  • Unplug, move your car, and tap I've unplugged.
  • Someone's car still there when it's your turn? Tap Nudge and they get a polite ping.
  • Want a calendar reminder by email? Turn it on in ChargeBot's Home tab.

🤝 Good charging manners

  • Confirm quickly, and skip if your plans change.
  • Move your car when your time's up, even if it isn't full.
  • Tap I'm done when you leave early. Someone's waiting.
